Python Basics & Beyond Final Project: Statistics Dashboard with Quarto

Author

The GRAPH Courses

Overview

For your final project, you will build a multi-page Quarto dashboard that explores and visualizes real-world data. This is an opportunity to consolidate your skills in data manipulation, visualization, and dashboard creation, while creating a portfolio-worthy project that you can share with future employers.

Special instruction for UNODA/BWC-sponsored students

**If you are sponsored by the UNODA Biological Weapons Convention (BWC) programme, your final project must include at least one health-security or biosecurity-relevant Gapminder indicator from this preset list: Gapminder biosecurity-relevant variables.

More details about this are described below.

If you are not sure whether this applies to you, this usually means students on a 100% scholarship/ who did not pay for the course.**

Checkpoint & Feedback Session (Dec 7)

The checkpoint session will be dedicated to getting feedback and mentorship on your project. By then, you should have at least one page of your dashboard ready. You will briefly present that page during the session; this presentation may factor into your grade.

Project Requirements

Data Selection

  • Choose at least two indicators from Gapminder’s data repository.
  • UNODA/BWC-sponsored students: at least one of your indicators must be a health-security or biosecurity-relevant indicator from this list: Gapminder biosecurity-relevant variables. These indicators come from the full Gapminder dataset and are available by country and year.
  • You can subset to a single year or include multiple years in your analysis.
  • If you would rather work with alternative datasets, perhaps those more relevant to your work, send an email to us at trainingteam@thegraphnetwork.org with your proposal. As long as your final dashboard meets the rubric requirements below, it could be a good alternative. If you are a UNODA/BWC-sponsored student, please also explain how your proposed dataset keeps the project relevant to health security or biosecurity.

Dashboard Structure

Your dashboard should contain at least three pages, with at least the following:

  1. First Indicator Analysis
  2. Second Indicator Analysis
  3. Relationship between the two indicators. No statistical analysis is required; this can be purely descriptive/graphical.

Technical Requirements

  • The dashboard should include at least 12 display elements.
  • Elements include:
    • Value boxes
    • Interactive plots
    • Tables
    • Maps
    • Text boxes with descriptions of key insights
  • You should demonstrate your ability to use a range of pandas methods/functions. (Polars or Ibis are also acceptable.)
  • You should demonstrate your ability to create a range of plotly visualizations. (Matplotlib, plotnine, seaborn, etc. are also acceptable.)

Checkpoint Presentation

During the checkpoint session (Dec 7), you will give a short live walkthrough of the page(s) you have ready so far. Be prepared to explain your data choices, visualizations, and any code decisions. This presentation may factor into your grade.

If you cannot attend your checkpoint session: email us at trainingteam@thegraphnetwork.org with a brief explanation, and record a short video (for example with Loom or any screen-recording tool) walking through your work-in-progress. Post the video link on the submission page when you submit.

We recommend covering:

  1. Brief introduction: What data did you choose and why?
  2. Live demo: Walk through the page you have ready, highlighting key visualizations and insights
  3. Next steps: What you still plan to add before final submission

Submission Instructions

  1. Upload your Quarto file (.qmd) to the submission page.
  2. Provide a link to the deployed dashboard on GitHub Pages.
  3. Present during the checkpoint session (at least one page), or, if you cannot attend, follow the alternate steps in Checkpoint Presentation.

Grading Rubric (100 points)

Data Selection and Manipulation (20 points)

  • Relevant data selected from Gapminder or approved alternative source
  • For UNODA/BWC-sponsored students, includes at least one approved health-security or biosecurity-relevant indicator
  • Data files are properly cleaned without errors or inconsistencies
  • Datasets are correctly merged when needed
  • Shows evidence of mastery of a range of pandas methods/functions

Dashboard Structure (15 points)

  • Contains required three pages (first indicator, second indicator, relationship)
  • Layout is logical, easy to navigate, and makes effective use of space
  • Design choices enhance understanding of the content

Visualization Elements (25 points)

  • Dashboard includes at least 12 display elements (value boxes, plots, tables, maps, etc.)
  • Plots have clear titles, labels, and appropriate scales
  • Interactive features (e.g., hover-over text) function correctly and display clear and useful information
  • Visual elements have titles or text boxes that explain the key insights
  • Appropriate choice of visualization types for the data

Code Quality (10 points)

  • Code is well-organized and readable
  • Code runs without errors
  • Efficient use of functions and operations
  • Consistent formatting and style

Deployment and Structure (10 points)

  • Dashboard is successfully deployed to GitHub Pages
  • Files are logically organized in repository
  • Repository contains only necessary files

Checkpoint Presentation (10 points)

  • Clear walkthrough of at least one completed dashboard page
  • Able to explain code logic and design choices when asked
  • Demonstrates genuine understanding of the tools and techniques used

Originality, Creativity, and Insight (10 points)

  • Original approaches to dashboard design, visualization, or interaction
  • Demonstrates thoughtful exploration of the data
  • Presents interesting patterns or relationships

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use similar data from my R class?

Yes.

Can I use AI?

Yes, but make sure you can explain and defend the code.

Should the two indicators be related?

Yes.

Can I use non-Gapminder data?

Yes, but send an email to trainingteam@thegraphnetwork.org first with your proposal.

What if my dashboard doesn’t work perfectly?

That’s okay! At the checkpoint, be clear about what is still in progress and what you would improve. Honest reflection on limitations is valued.

How complete does my dashboard need to be for the checkpoint?

You should have at least one page ready to present. Other pages can still be in progress. Be clear about what’s preliminary and what your next steps will be.

What kind of questions will be asked?

The questions are designed to check that you understand your own code. For example: “Why did you choose this type of plot here?” or “How did you merge these two datasets?”

What if I miss the checkpoint session?

See Checkpoint Presentation for the alternate process (email with a brief reason and a recorded walkthrough).
